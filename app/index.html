The road to the Platinum

Roughly 200 hours from first mission to Platinum, per PowerPyx. Seven stages, in the order that wastes the least time.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story blind

    Chapters 1–4 and the final mission earn 13 trophies on their own. Do not chase collectibles yet; just finish the campaign and enjoy it.

  2. Stage 2 — The one missable spot

    Before Chapter 1 ends, meditate at its meditation spot. PowerPyx and GTABase flag Positive Mental Attitude as the only trophy that may not come back — do this one early.

  3. Stage 3 — Stranger strands

    Finish the stranger chains for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret, plus two more, for Strangers & Freaks and five single-strand trophies.

  4. Stage 4 — Activities & races

    Stunts (250), Shooting Range golds (42), drone races (14), 20 race wins and 50 challenges. These overlap heavily — do them in the same sessions.

  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles & exploration

    WAiNK scans (100), card sets (3), vehicles (100), all 52 areas of Leonida and the 18 meditation spots. Pair with the money trophies below.

  6. Stage 6 — Crime & money

    Bank vaults (5), armored truck, shop robberies with your partner, gang operations (9), laundering $250,000 and spending $100,000.

  7. Stage 7 — The 100% checklist

    After the story, the 35-task 100% Completion Checklist unlocks. Clear it for A Long Road, then mop up anything left. The Platinum pops last.
